Master the Occupational English Test

The Occupational English Test (OET) assesses the language communication skills of healthcare professionals who seek to register and practice in an English-speaking environment.

Who is this course for?

This course is specifically tailored for healthcare professionals, including nurses, doctors, dentists, and pharmacists, aiming to work in countries like the UK, Australia, New Zealand, and Ireland.

What You Will Learn

  • Listening: Understanding patient consultations and healthcare presentations.
  • Reading: Comprehending medical texts, journals, and workplace guidelines.
  • Writing: Drafting clear, accurate referral letters and healthcare documentation.
  • Speaking: Role-playing real clinical scenarios with confidence and clarity.

Our Training Approach

Our OET training utilizes authentic healthcare scenarios and role-plays. We provide profession-specific preparation to ensure the context of your learning is highly relevant to your clinical practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is OET?

OET is an international English language test that assesses the language proficiency of healthcare professionals seeking to register and practice in an English-speaking environment.

Is OET suitable for all healthcare professionals?

OET provides specific tests for 12 different healthcare professions, including Medicine, Nursing, Dentistry, Pharmacy, and Physiotherapy, ensuring the test material is highly relevant.

What are the four OET sub-tests?

The test comprises four sub-tests: Listening (approx. 45 minutes), Reading (60 minutes), Writing (45 minutes), and Speaking (approx. 20 minutes).

How is OET different from IELTS?

While both assess English proficiency, OET tests language in a healthcare context. Tasks simulate real workplace scenarios, such as writing a referral letter or consulting a patient, unlike the general topics found in IELTS.
