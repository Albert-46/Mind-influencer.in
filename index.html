Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer one-to-one classes?

Yes, we focus on personalised training and offer one-to-one options to ensure you get the exact guidance you need.

Can I attend classes online?

Yes, we offer online classes. Please contact us via WhatsApp to discuss available schedules.

Do you provide a free demo?

Absolutely. We offer a free assessment and demo class so you can experience our approach before enrolling.

Which IELTS and OET preparation options do you support?

We provide training for all modules (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) focusing on Study, Work, and Migration pathways.

Do you train healthcare professionals for OET?

Yes, we provide practical OET coaching specifically tailored for nurses and healthcare professionals.

What German levels do you teach?

We provide comprehensive German language preparation for levels A1 through B2.

How do I choose the right course?

Book a free demo. We will assess your current level and goals, and guide you to the correct pathway.

How can I contact Mind Influencer | MIAFL?

You can reach us through the enquiry form below, chat with us on WhatsApp, email us at info@mindinfluencer.in, or call our office.
