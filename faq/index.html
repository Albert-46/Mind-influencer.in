Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you start your learning journey.

IELTS

What is the difference between IELTS Academic and General Training?

IELTS Academic is typically for individuals applying for higher education or professional registration abroad. IELTS General Training is for those going to English-speaking countries for secondary education, work experience, or migration.

What are the four IELTS sections?

The test consists of four sections: Listening (30 minutes), Reading (60 minutes), Writing (60 minutes), and Speaking (11-14 minutes).

OET

Is OET suitable for healthcare professionals?

Yes, OET is designed specifically for healthcare professionals. It tests language proficiency using real clinical scenarios relevant to 12 different healthcare professions.

How is OET different from IELTS?

OET focuses on healthcare-specific communication (like reading medical journals or writing referral letters), whereas IELTS covers general and academic topics.

German Language

What are A1, A2, B1 and B2?

These are proficiency levels based on the CEFR scale. A1 is absolute beginner, while B2 indicates an upper-intermediate ability to communicate fluently and independently.

What skills are developed while learning German?

Our training comprehensively builds your speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills to ensure balanced communication abilities.

Spoken English

How can I become more confident speaking English?

Building confidence requires regular practice in a supportive, judgment-free environment. Interactive role-plays and personalized feedback are key components of our approach.

How can I improve English pronunciation?

By listening to native speakers, practicing phonetics, and receiving direct, guided feedback from our trainers to correct consistent errors.
